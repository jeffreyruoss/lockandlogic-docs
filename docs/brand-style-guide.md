# Branding Style Guide

Your visual identity — colors, typography, and design language — all derived from the Lock & Logic logo and website. Use this guide to keep everything consistent across your site, social media, print materials, and marketing.

## Logo

The Lock & Logic logo combines a **shield** and **padlock** with interlocking **puzzle pieces**, representing security, mystery, and problem-solving. It comes in two versions:

| Version | Use |
|---------|-----|
| **Full logo** (icon + wordmark) | Website footer, social media profiles, printed materials, email signatures |
| **Icon only** (shield/padlock) | Website header, favicons, app icons, small spaces where the full logo won't fit |

::: warning Logo Rules
- Always use the official logo files — never recreate or modify the logo
- Maintain clear space around the logo (at least the width of the "L" in "Lock")
- Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the logo
- On dark backgrounds, use the logo as-is (it's designed for dark)
- On light backgrounds (print, documents), the logo works without modification
:::

## Typography

### Fonts

| Font | Weight | Role |
|------|--------|------|
| **Barlow Condensed** | 600 (Semi-Bold), 700 (Bold) | Headings — all uppercase with wide letter-spacing |
| **Inter** | Variable (100–900) | Body text, UI elements, everything else |

### Heading Style

All headings use **Barlow Condensed**, **uppercase**, with **0.05em letter-spacing**. This creates a bold, mechanical feel that matches the escape room aesthetic.

### Body Style

Body text uses **Inter** at regular weight in **Steel 200** (`#c8cdd8`). Inter is a clean, highly readable sans-serif designed for screens.

### Text Selection

When users select text on the site, the highlight uses **Gold 400** (`#d4a843`) background with **Navy 950** (`#030d19`) text — reinforcing the brand even in small interactions.

## Visual Effects

The website uses subtle glow and gradient effects to create an immersive, atmospheric feel.

### Gold Gradient Text

Used for hero text and key headings. Flows from Gold 300 through Gold 400 to Gold 500 at a 135-degree angle.

### Glow Effects

- **Gold glow** — applied to primary headings and CTAs. A layered text-shadow that creates a warm, lantern-like radiance.
- **Teal glow** — applied to secondary headings and interactive elements. A cooler, more subtle glow.

### Design Atmosphere

The overall design targets a **dark, immersive, premium** feel:
- Deep navy backgrounds create depth
- Gold accents signal quality and mystery
- Teal accents add visual variety without competing with gold
- Subtle animations (particle effects, ken burns on images) add life without distraction
- Card-elevated shadows create layered depth

## Usage Guidelines

### Social Media

- **Profile picture:** Icon logo (shield/padlock)
- **Cover photos:** Use Navy 950 background with Gold 400 accents
- **Post backgrounds:** Navy 900 or Navy 800 for variety
- **Text on images:** Steel 100 for headings, Steel 200 for body
- **Hashtag/link color:** Teal 400

### Print Materials

- Use the full logo on business cards, flyers, and posters
- Gold 400 works well as a spot color on dark print backgrounds
- For light-background print (receipts, forms), use Navy 950 text with Gold 500 accents
- Headings in Barlow Condensed, body in Inter

### Email

- Keep the dark theme for marketing emails (Navy 950 background)
- Use Gold 400 for CTA buttons
- Body text in Steel 200 on dark, or dark gray on light backgrounds
