# Google Business Video Verification

Instructions for recording the video verification required by Google to verify your business listing.

📎 [Google Business Profile Help — Video verification](https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705?hl=en)

---

::: danger 🎥 Important
The entire video must be **one continuous shot** -- do not stop or pause the recording at any point.
:::

::: info 👤 Who Records
**Mark or Dan** (owners) must be the one holding the phone and appearing on camera.
:::

---

## Step-by-Step

| Step | Action | Details |
|:----:|--------|---------|
| 🏙️ | **Start outside** | Begin recording outside the building. Show nearby street signs and neighboring businesses to establish the location. |
| 🪧 | **Show the Lock & Logic sign** | Walk toward the building and capture the business sign clearly. |
| 🔢 | **Show the address number** | Get a clear shot of the address number on the door. |
| 🔑 | **Unlock the door** | Unlock and open the door on camera to prove access to the premises. |
| 🏠 | **Show the inside** | Walk inside and show the interior of the business. |
| 🙋 | **Show yourself** | Spin the phone around to show the person recording (Mark or Dan). |
| 📄 | **Show a legal document** | Hold up a document that shows both the **person's name** and the **business name** in the same shot. |

---

## Accepted Documents

::: tip 📋 What to bring
The document needs to clearly show the **person's name** and the **business name** together. Any of these will work:
:::

| Document | Example |
|----------|---------|
| 📑 Board resolution | Appointing directors, authorizing bank access |
| 📜 Articles of incorporation | Filed with the state |
| 🏛️ Business license | Issued by local/state government |
| 💡 Utility bill | In the business name at the business address |
| 🛡️ Insurance policy | In the business name |
