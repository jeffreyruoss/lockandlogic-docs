# Google Business Video Verification

Instructions for recording the video verification required by Google to verify your business listing.

---

## Key Rules

- **One continuous shot** -- Do not stop or pause the recording at any point.
- **Recorded by an owner** -- Mark or Dan must be the one holding the phone and appearing on camera.

---

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start outside** -- Begin recording outside the building. Show nearby street signs and neighboring businesses to establish the location.
2. **Show the Lock & Logic sign** -- Walk toward the building and capture the business sign clearly.
3. **Show the address number** -- Get a clear shot of the address number on the door.
4. **Unlock the door** -- Unlock and open the door on camera to prove access to the premises.
5. **Show the inside** -- Walk inside and show the interior of the business.
6. **Show yourself** -- Spin the phone around to show the person recording (Mark or Dan).
7. **Show a legal document** -- Hold up a legal document that shows both the **person's name** (Mark or Dan) and the **business name** (Lock & Logic) in the same shot.

---

## Accepted Documents

The document needs to clearly show the **person's name** and the **business name** together. Any of these will work:

- Board resolutions (e.g., appointing directors, authorizing bank access)
- Articles of incorporation
- Business license
- Utility bill in the business name
- Insurance policy in the business name
