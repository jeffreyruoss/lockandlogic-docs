# Google Analytics AI Integration

Your Google Analytics account is connected to an AI-powered reporting system. Instead of navigating the GA4 dashboard to find data, you can ask plain English questions and get answers instantly.

## What You Can Ask

Here are some examples of questions the system can answer:

- **"How many visitors did we get this week?"**
- **"What are our top traffic sources for the last 30 days?"**
- **"How is the flyer QR code campaign performing?"**
- **"Show me sessions broken down by source and medium"**
- **"Which pages get the most views?"**
- **"How many users came from Facebook this month?"**
- **"What's our daily traffic trend for the past 2 weeks?"**

The system translates your question into a Google Analytics query and returns the data — no dashboard navigation required.

## What's Connected

The integration has **read-only** access to your Google Analytics data. It can see everything you'd normally see in GA4 reports:

- **Traffic sources** — where visitors come from (direct, social media, search, QR codes, etc.)
- **Campaign tracking** — performance of any marketing campaign using UTM parameters (like the flyer QR codes)
- **User metrics** — total users, new users, returning users, sessions
- **Page performance** — which pages get the most views
- **Geography** — where your visitors are located
- **Device data** — desktop vs mobile vs tablet

It cannot modify your analytics settings, change your website, or access any personal information about individual visitors.

## QR Code Campaign Tracking

Your print marketing materials can include QR codes with built-in tracking. When someone scans a QR code, the visit is automatically tagged with the source, medium, and campaign name — so you can see exactly which materials are driving traffic.

See [QR Code Campaigns](/qr-campaigns) for details on active campaigns and how to add new ones.

## How to Use It

This integration runs through your developer's AI assistant. When you want to check analytics, just ask — your developer can pull the data in seconds and share the results with you. No GA4 login needed on your end.
