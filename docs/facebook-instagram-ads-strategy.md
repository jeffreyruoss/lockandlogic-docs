# Lock & Logic — Facebook & Instagram Ads Strategy


This document outlines a paid social media advertising strategy for Lock & Logic on Facebook and Instagram (both run through Meta Ads Manager). Unlike Google Ads, which captures people already searching for escape rooms, social ads put your business in front of people who aren't searching yet — but are exactly the type of person who would book.

---

## Why Facebook & Instagram Ads for Lock & Logic

Google Ads catches people with intent. Social ads create demand. Both are valuable, but they work differently.

- **Visual product, visual platforms.** Escape rooms are inherently exciting and photogenic. Images and videos of themed rooms, group celebrations, and puzzle close-ups perform extremely well on social media.
- **Precise audience targeting.** Meta lets you target by location, age, interests, behaviors, and life events — you can reach people in Pottstown who are interested in entertainment, puzzles, or group activities.
- **Event-driven bookings.** A huge percentage of escape room bookings are tied to events — birthdays, date nights, corporate outings, holiday activities. Social ads can target people approaching these moments.
- **Awareness in a new market.** Most people in Pottstown don't know Lock & Logic exists yet. Social ads build awareness at scale in a way that search ads can't.
- **Low cost of entry.** Facebook and Instagram ads can drive results with budgets as low as $5-10/day, making them accessible from day one.
- **Remarketing synergy.** The Meta Pixel tracks website visitors, letting you retarget people who browsed your site but didn't book — across both Facebook and Instagram.

## Part 1: Audience Strategy

The power of Facebook and Instagram ads is in the targeting.

### Core Audiences (Interest & Demographic Targeting)

| Audience | Targeting Details | Use Case |
|----------|------------------|----------|
| **Local Activity Seekers** | 20-mile radius of Pottstown, ages 21-55, interests: escape rooms, puzzles, board games, adventure activities, entertainment | Primary booking campaign |
| **Date Night Couples** | 15-mile radius, ages 25-45, relationship status: in a relationship or married, interests: date night, romantic activities, restaurants | Date night promotions |
| **Birthday Planners** | 20-mile radius, ages 25-50, interests: birthday party planning, party supplies, kids activities | Birthday party packages |
| **Corporate / HR** | 25-mile radius, ages 28-55, job titles: HR manager, office manager, team lead, event coordinator; interests: team building, corporate events | Team building campaigns |
| **Parents** | 15-mile radius, ages 30-50, parents of teens (13-17) or young adults, interests: family activities, things to do with kids | Family escape room experiences |
| **Young Adults** | 15-mile radius, ages 18-30, interests: gaming, puzzles, escape rooms, group activities, social events | Weekend activity promotions |

### Custom Audiences (Your Own Data)

Custom audiences are built from people who have already interacted with your business — your warmest prospects.

| Audience | Source | Use Case |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **Website visitors (all)** | Meta Pixel — last 30 days | General remarketing |
| **Room page viewers** | Meta Pixel — viewed room pages | Room-specific remarketing |
| **Booking abandoners** | Meta Pixel — initiated checkout but no purchase | High-intent remarketing |
| **Past customers** | Customer email list from Resova | Upsell other rooms, gift cards, bring-a-friend offers |
| **Email subscribers** | Email list | Nurture with special offers |
| **Social engagers** | Engaged with Facebook Page or Instagram in last 90 days | Warm audience for promotions |
| **Video viewers** | Watched 50%+ of a video ad | Interest-based remarketing |

### Lookalike Audiences

Once you have enough customer data (typically 100+ purchases tracked by the Pixel), Meta can find new people who "look like" your best customers.

| Lookalike Source | Size | Use Case |
|-----------------|------|----------|
| Past customers (purchases) | 1% | Highest-quality prospecting — people most similar to actual bookers |
| Website visitors | 1-2% | Broader prospecting based on site interest |
| Social engagers | 2-3% | Widest net for awareness campaigns |

Start with 1% lookalikes (the top 1% most similar people in your target area) and expand to 2-3% as you scale.

## Part 3: Ad Creative

Social ads live or die on creative. The best targeting in the world won't matter if the ad doesn't stop someone from scrolling.

### What Works for Escape Rooms

| Creative Type | Performance | Notes |
|---------------|-------------|-------|
| **Short-form video (15-30 sec)** | Highest | Room walkthroughs, team reactions, countdown timer clips. Video drives 2-3x more engagement than static images. |
| **Carousel (multiple images)** | High | Show all rooms in one ad. Each card = one room with a "Book Now" button. |
| **UGC (user-generated content)** | High | Real customer photos and videos feel authentic. Repost with permission. |
| **Single image** | Medium | Works for simple promotions, gift cards, and event-specific ads. |
| **Stories/Reels format** | High | Full-screen vertical video (9:16). The fastest-growing ad format. |

Show real rooms and real people — not stock photos. Include faces (ads with faces get 38% more engagement). Create separate creative for Stories/Reels (9:16 vertical) and Feed (1:1 square or 4:5). Never give away room puzzles or solutions.

## Part 4: Budget Recommendations

Facebook and Instagram ads are generally cheaper than Google Ads, especially for awareness and engagement campaigns.

### Expected Costs

| Metric | Expected Range |
|--------|---------------|
| **CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions)** | $5 – $15 |
| **CPC (cost per click)** | $0.50 – $2.00 |
| **Cost per lead** | $3 – $8 |
| **Cost per booking** | $8 – $20 |
| **Video view (ThruPlay)** | $0.02 – $0.08 |

### Recommended Monthly Budgets

| Phase | Monthly Budget | Daily Budget | Notes |
|-------|---------------|-------------|-------|
| **Pre-Launch** (March – April) | $300 – $500 | $10 – $17 | Awareness and anticipation building |
| **Launch** (May) | $600 – $1,000 | $20 – $33 | Maximum push for opening month |
| **Growth** (June – August) | $400 – $600 | $13 – $20 | Consistent booking campaigns + remarketing |
| **Steady state** (September+) | $300 – $500 | $10 – $17 | Ongoing with seasonal boosts |
| **Seasonal peaks** (October, December) | $500 – $800 | $17 – $27 | Halloween, holiday gift cards |

### Budget Allocation by Campaign Type

| Campaign Type | % of Budget |
|---------------|------------|
| Conversion — Book Now | 35% |
| Remarketing | 20% |
| Events (date night, birthday, corporate) | 20% |
| Awareness / Video Views | 15% |
| Testing new creative | 10% |

At a $500/month budget, you can expect roughly **250-500 website clicks** and **15-40 bookings** (at $12-$20 cost per booking), generating **$1,500-$4,800 in booking revenue** from a $500 investment.

## Part 5: Pre-Launch Campaign (March – April 2026)

The pre-launch period is about building anticipation and growing an audience before opening day.

### Campaign: "Coming Soon" Awareness

**Objective:** Reach / Video Views

**Audience:** 20-mile radius of Pottstown, ages 18-55, interests in entertainment, activities, escape rooms, puzzles

**Creative ideas:**

| Content | Format | Purpose |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Room teaser video (construction progress → finished room reveal) | Video (15-30 sec) | Build excitement, show this is real and coming soon |
| "Coming May 2026" announcement graphic | Image | Simple awareness |
| Behind-the-scenes puzzle construction | Reels / Stories | Create intrigue and investment in the brand |
| "What kind of escape room player are you?" quiz/poll | Stories interactive | Drive engagement and grow followers |
| Countdown posts (30 days, 2 weeks, 1 week) | Carousel or image | Build urgency and anticipation |

### Campaign: Email / Early Access Signup

**Objective:** Leads

**Audience:** Same as above, plus people who engaged with awareness content

**Offer:** "Be the first to book. Sign up for early access and get [10% off / first dibs on opening-week time slots]."

An email list of 200-500 local people ready to book on opening day is extremely valuable. Even at a $5 cost per lead, spending $500-1,000 to build this list pays for itself many times over.

## Part 6: Launch Campaign (May 2026)

Opening month is your biggest push. The goal is maximum visibility in the Pottstown area.

### Campaign: Grand Opening

**Objective:** Conversions (Purchase)

**Audience:** Email list (early access), local interest audiences, social engagers from pre-launch

| Ad | Format | Copy Hook |
|----|--------|-----------|
| "We're Open" announcement | Video + Image | "Pottstown's first escape room is officially open. Book your adventure today." |
| Room showcase carousel | Carousel | Each card highlights one room with a photo, name, difficulty, and "Book Now" CTA |
| Opening week special | Image | "Opening week special: [offer]. Book now — limited slots available." |
| Customer reaction video | Video (Reels) | First customers celebrating after escaping — authentic excitement |

### Campaign: Social Proof Push

As soon as the first groups play, capture their experience:

- Post group celebration photos (with permission) and boost them as ads
- Share first Google/Facebook reviews as quote graphics
- Video testimonials from happy groups ("What did you think?")

Social proof is the single most powerful conversion driver for escape rooms. Real people having a great time is more convincing than any ad copy you can write.

## Part 7: Measuring Success

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| **ROAS (return on ad spend)** | 4:1 or better |
| **Cost per booking** | Under $20 |
| **Click-through rate (CTR)** | 1-3% (Feed), 0.5-1.5% (Stories) |
| **Cost per click (CPC)** | Under $2.00 |
| **Frequency** | Under 3.0 (over 3 = ad fatigue, refresh creative) |
| **Conversion rate** | 3-8% |

Review performance weekly by campaign, creative, and audience. Do a full funnel analysis monthly and a strategy review quarterly. Refresh creative at least monthly — never let the same ads run more than 4-6 weeks.
