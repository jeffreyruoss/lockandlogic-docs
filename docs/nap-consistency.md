# 🏷️ NAP Consistency

**NAP** stands for **Name, Address, Phone number** — the core identity details that appear wherever your business is listed online. For local search and AI visibility, these should be defined in **one canonical format and used identically everywhere** — your website, Google Business Profile, every directory listing, and all social profiles.

## Why It Matters

Search engines and AI assistants build confidence that a business is real, and resolve "Lock & Logic" to one specific entity, by matching these details across the web. Even small inconsistencies — "Street" vs. "St.", "Unit 1" vs. "Ste 1", a phone number formatted two different ways — make that matching less reliable and can quietly weaken your local rankings and your chances of being cited in AI answers.

It's one of the highest-leverage, lowest-effort pieces of local SEO: pick one format, use it verbatim, never improvise.

## The Canonical Format for Lock & Logic

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Name** | Lock & Logic |
| **Address** | 142 Shoemaker Rd, Unit 1, Pottstown, PA 19464 |
| **Phone** | (610) 819-0152 |
| **Canonical URL** | `https://www.lockandlogic.com` (always with `www`) |
| **Email** | info@lockandlogic.com |

Copy these exactly. Don't abbreviate "Road" in one place and spell it out in another, and don't reformat the phone number.

## "Lock & Logic" vs. "Lock & Logic, LLC"

Use **"Lock & Logic"** (no "LLC") for **every customer-facing citation** — Google Business Profile, website, schema, social profiles, directories, and press. Google's guidelines require the real-world, customer-facing name (what's on your signage), not the legal entity name; adding "LLC" can get the listing flagged or edited.

Reserve **"Lock & Logic, LLC"** for **legal and billing contexts only** — contracts, invoices, payment processors, tax and insurance paperwork, and any directory's separate "legal name" field (distinct from the public display name).

## Where It Needs to Match

- Website (footer, contact page, structured data)
- Google Business Profile
- Apple Maps, Bing Places
- Facebook, Instagram, and any other social profiles
- Escape room directories (Morty, Room Escape Artist, Escaperoom.com, World of Escapes)
- Local and general directories (Yelp, TripAdvisor, Chamber of Commerce, Yellow Pages, Foursquare)
- Press mentions and partner listings

## Keeping It Consistent

- **Audit quarterly** — search for the business name and spot-check that listings still match (listings sometimes get auto-edited or pulled from third-party data).
- **Fix mismatches at the source** — update the directory directly rather than creating a duplicate listing.
- **One phone number** — use the same number everywhere; avoid tracking numbers that differ per listing, as they break NAP matching.
