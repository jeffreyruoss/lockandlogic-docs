# Room Schedule

Daily time slot schedule for all four core rooms.

::: warning Provisional — pending client confirmation
This is a working draft. **Updated June 28, 2026:** the Monday–Friday opening was moved earlier (to noon) as a placeholder to recover capacity — **final operating hours are still pending client confirmation** and will change the slot times and totals below. The **30-minute reset** between bookings (set June 25, up from 15) is reflected throughout.
:::

- **Game duration:** 60 minutes
- **Reset time:** 30 minutes between sessions (up from 15)
- **Per-room cycle:** 90 minutes (60-minute game + 30-minute reset)
- **Stagger:** Each room starts 15 minutes after the previous one
- **First game:** 12:00 PM every day (Mon–Fri opening is a placeholder pending confirmation)
- **Cost:** $35/person (tax included)
- **Players:** 2–8 per room

**A note on closing time:** because the four rooms are staggered 15 minutes apart, the last room (The Forgotten God) starts at 9:45 PM and finishes at **10:45 PM**. So while the booking window closes around 10:00 PM, the building stays occupied until roughly 10:45 PM — operating hours need to extend past the final game. The exact open/close still needs the client's sign-off.
