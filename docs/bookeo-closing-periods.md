# Bookeo Closing Periods

> How to close a day (or date range) in Bookeo so no one can book it.

---

## When to use this

- A day has no bookings and you want to close early / skip the day entirely
- A planned closure (holiday, vacation, maintenance, private event)
- Any time you need to stop new bookings on a specific date

Once a closing period is saved, the date is immediately blocked on your public booking page. If a customer selects that date, they'll see the message:

> There are no Rooms on this date, for the options selected.

---

## How to add a closing period

1. In Bookeo, go to **Settings → Closing periods**.
2. Click **+ New period**.
3. Choose the date (or date range).
4. Click **Save**.

::: warning Important — don't skip this step
After clicking **Save**, it *looks* like the closing period is saved, but it isn't yet. You have to click the **Ok** button back on the **Closing periods** screen to actually apply it.

If you navigate away without clicking **Ok**, the closing period will be lost.
:::

---

## How to remove a closing period

1. Go to **Settings → Closing periods**.
2. Find the period in the list and remove it.
3. Click **Ok** to apply the change (same gotcha as above).

---

## Note on existing bookings

Closing periods only block **new** bookings. If a customer has already booked that day, their booking is not cancelled automatically — you'll need to contact them directly to reschedule or refund.
