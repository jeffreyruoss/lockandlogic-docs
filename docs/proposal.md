# Project Proposal

A complete digital package for **Lock & Logic** escape rooms — not just a website, but online booking, a branding style guide, all marketing copywriting, SEO foundation, automated email systems, and 3 months of post-launch support.

::: info At a Glance
- **Price:** $4,000 flat
- **Timeline:** Launch Mid June 2026
:::

## What's Included

### Core Website & Design

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Atmospheric design** | Dark, immersive design that signals quality. Your website is the first impression of your experience. |
| **Branding style guide** | Colors, typography, and visual language defined from your logo and website design — keeps everything consistent across your site and marketing. |
| **Room pages** | Each room gets its own page with photos, description, difficulty, group size, duration, and price. |
| **Clear pricing** | Prices visible upfront. Hidden pricing is the #1 reason people leave escape room websites without booking. |
| **Mobile-friendly design** | Works perfectly on all devices. Over 60% of visitors will be on their phones. |
| **Custom 404 page** | Branded error page with helpful links instead of a dead end. |

### Content & Accessibility

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Website copywriting** | All copy for the site — room descriptions, page content, calls to action. You're welcome to submit your own or request changes at any time. |
| **How it works section** | Explanation for first-timers: what to expect, how it works, what to bring, when to arrive. Reduces anxiety, increases bookings. |
| **Location & contact info** | Address, phone, email, hours, and embedded Google Map. |
| **Contact form** | Simple form for general inquiries (group events, questions, partnerships) via Formspree or similar. Separate from booking. |
| **FAQ page** | Answers common pre-booking questions. Removes hesitation. |
| **Accessibility info** | Wheelchair access and physical considerations per room. Builds trust, avoids surprises. |
| **Legal pages** | Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Policy. Required by law for any site collecting data and processing payments. |

### Booking & Operations

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Online booking** | Customers pick a room, date/time, and pay online. No back-and-forth calls or emails. |
| **Digital waivers** | Online liability waivers signed before arrival. Better experience, less paperwork at check-in. |
| **Booking data backups** | Automatic sync to Google Sheets via Zapier or similar. Protects against platform outages or data loss. |
| **Email capture during booking** | Opt-in checkbox for your Mailchimp list. Builds your marketing list automatically from every booking. |

### Marketing & Engagement

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Newsletter signup** | An email list you own for promotions, new rooms, and events. Most cost-effective marketing channel. |
| **Automated follow-up emails** | Post-visit emails with thank-you, review request, discount, or referral offer. Turns one-time visitors into repeat customers. |
| **Automated email reminders** | Confirmation on booking, reminders before visit, follow-up after. Reduces no-shows. |
| **Reviews page and homepage section** | Dedicated reviews page plus homepage testimonial section. Social proof helps with SEO. |
| **Automated review requests** | Post-visit emails requesting Google reviews. Fresh reviews improve local search ranking. |

### SEO & Visibility

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---------|---------------------|
| **SEO foundation** | Technical setup for "escape room near me" and local searches. Without this, you're invisible online. |
| **Analytics** | Visitor tracking — traffic sources, drop-off points, conversions. You can't improve what you don't measure. |
| **Structured data** | Code that tells Google to show star ratings, prices, and hours directly in search results. Most competitors don't do this. |
| **Google Business Profile** | Your listing on Google Maps and Search — what people see first when searching "escape room near me." |

## A Note on Third-Party Platforms

This project integrates with Bookeo and Houdini MC — both third-party platforms with their own limitations. If we run into a constraint, I'll communicate it clearly and work with you on the best path forward.

## What You'll Be Responsible For

- **Bookeo account** — Create the initial account with your email address and billing info. I'll handle configuration and integration from there.
- **Stripe account** — Recommended payment processor for Bookeo. Create the initial account with your email address and connect your bank info. I'll connect it to Bookeo.
- **Windows PC for Houdini MC** — Dedicated Windows computer at your venue. This is the central hub — game masters connect from iPads/tablets on the local network.
- **Houdini MC hardware setup** — Physical setup at your venue (HDMI cables to room TVs, mounting displays, etc.) needs to be handled on-site.
